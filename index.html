04 TRAK

TRAK when one month isn't enough.

A TRAK is its own spending line for something that stretches across months or currencies—a trip abroad, tuition, an upcoming wedding. Log entries in local currency; Skie converts them to your app's base currency. When you set one up, you can keep it out of your monthly ledger so everyday totals stay clear.

  • Any currency On a trip, log in yen, euros, or whatever you're spending. Each amount converts to your base currency automatically.
  • Across months Weddings, tuition, renovations—follow the real cost of something that doesn't fit a single billing cycle.
  • Separate ledger Exclude a TRAK from your monthly totals when you set it up, so special spending doesn't blur your day-to-day habits.

05 Why Manual?

The friction is the feature.

Studies show manual tracking builds better financial habits. When you log each purchase, you notice patterns—and make intentional choices.
Notice
Each entry is a quiet pause that surfaces what you'd otherwise miss.
Reflect

Decide

06 Privacy

Your data stays with you.

Skie is built around a simple promise: your spending is your business. We do not sync to external accounts. We do not share your data.

  • ✕No bank account connections, ever.
  • ✕No third-party analytics on your entries.
  • ✕No cloud backup unless you choose it.
  • ✕No ads. No data resale. No exceptions.
