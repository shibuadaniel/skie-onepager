01 Purpose

Read once. Remember it later.

The paragraph that struck you gets lost in the pages around it. Marginalia gives it a place to land, so you can catalogue what you read and recall it when you need it.

02 Features

From the page to your catalogue.

Capture the paragraph, file it with its source, and find it again when the idea comes back.

01 · Send

Send just the paragraph.

Select the passage that matters and add it to Marginalia with the source attached—without copying the whole page.

02 · Inbox

New passages start in your inbox.

Fresh saves land here first. Review them, then file into your catalogue or clear what you do not need.

03 · Catalogue

Filed with where it came from.

Each passage keeps its source—book, article, or page—so your library reflects what you actually read.

04 · Recall

Find it when it matters again.

Search your library or browse by source when a line you saved weeks ago is suddenly useful.

03 How it works

Select, send, recall.

From a line on the page to a passage you can find again.

SelectHighlight or paste the paragraph worth keeping, with enough context to remember why.
SendAdd it in the web app. It appears in your inbox with the source attached.
RecallFile it in your catalogue, then search or browse when you need it.
04 Privacy

What you read stays yours.

What you save reflects what you read. Marginalia keeps that library on your account and out of ad networks.

  • Your passages are visible only to you when you are signed in.
  • No third-party analytics on what you save.
  • No ads. No resale of your reading data.
  • Delete passages or your account when you choose.
