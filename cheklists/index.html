01Purpose

Start from a list. Finish the work.

A checklist is the plan. ChekLists turns it into tasks with due dates, so the plan becomes something you work through and check off, every time it comes around.

For teams

Everyone on the same list.

Invite people, group them into teams, and give each person a role: administrator, team lead, team member or executive.

For individuals

Your own lists, your own pace.

Personal mode keeps your checklists and your task list to yourself.

02Features

Small lists, checked off.

For the jobs that come back every day, week, month or year: opening up, inspections, reviews, upkeep.

01 · Build

Build a checklist,
or bring one in.

Write one from scratch or import a list you already use. A ready-made checklist library is coming soon.

02 · Repeat

Daily, weekly,
monthly, yearly.

Set how often a checklist runs and its tasks come due on schedule, with no copying it out again.

03 · My Tasks

Everything you owe,
in one list.

My Tasks gathers your tasks from every checklist, grouped by checklist or by status, with what's due today on top.

04 · Track

See what's done

The dashboard shows active checklists, today's completions, what's overdue and the completion rate for each list.

03How it works

Three steps, every time.

  1. 1Build or import a checklist

    Write down the steps once, the way the job should be done.

  2. 2Set how often it runs

    Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. Each run brings its tasks due.

  3. 3Check them off

    On your own, or with your team, and watch the completion rate climb.

04Your data

Where your lists live.

ChekLists syncs checklists and tasks so teams can share the same lists. Your data stays yours—and under your control.

  • Only people you invite can see a team’s checklists.
  • No ads and no selling of your task data.
  • Export or delete your lists when you need to.
