Everyone knows where you're going.

02 Features

Everything about the trip, in one place.

Built for the person who plans the trip and the people waiting at home.

01 · Trip

Every detail in one briefing.

Each trip has its own sections for flights, car rentals, stays, insurance and congregations, with a photo of the destination on your trip list.

02 · Files

Documents, kept private.

Upload tickets and bookings to a private store. Mark a document for approval, and you okay each guest before they open it.

03 · Share

Family in, no account needed.

Tap Share on a trip and add their email. They open it with a code sent to that inbox, and see your profile photo so they know it's from you.

04 · Passkey

One code, then just a passkey.

The first visit saves a passkey on that device. After that, opening the trip asks only for the passkey.

03 How it works

From plans to peace of mind.

You plan the trip. Your family opens it from their own inbox.

AddEnter the trip: places, dates, flights, stays and the files that go with them.
SharePut your family's emails on the trip's list and send the share link.
OpenThey enter the code sent to their inbox and save a passkey. Next time, the passkey is enough.
04 Privacy

Your trip, your list.

Trip details say where you'll be and when. Reveal lets only the people you choose see them, and lets you take that back.

  • Only emails on your list can open a trip, and only with a code sent to that inbox.
  • Revoke a share link or remove an email whenever you want.
  • Documents stay in a private store, and ones you mark for approval wait for your okay.
  • Share links and codes are stored as hashes, never as the link or code itself.
  • Delete a trip or your account at any time, or download a copy first.
